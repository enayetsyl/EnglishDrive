# Monday — First Teaching: singular & plural · the term *plural* · the base rule

**Day 1 of 2 teaching days** · 35 minutes · English throughout · Clue Card issued today
**Term strand today:** introduce **singular** and **plural** — first exposure.

| Step | Time | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–5 | Re-walk: what is a naming word? |
| 2 | 5–10 | The idea: one, and more than one |
| 3 | 10–14 | **Term: singular / plural** |
| 4 | 14–20 | The rule: most words add **s** |
| 5 | 20–26 | Words ending s, sh, ch, x → **es** |
| 6 | 26–30 | One changer: tooth → teeth · CW-1 out |
| 7 | 30–35 | Exit Check |

### Step 1 — Re-walk: what is a naming word? (0–5 min)

**Books closed.** Write: `book · bird · tree · bag · hen`

> "From Blocks 1 and 2 — what do we call these words?" → **Naming words. Nouns.**
> "A naming word names a person, an animal, a place, or a thing. Say it with me — *a naming word names something.*"
> "Today we look at naming words when there is **one**, and when there are **many**."

### Step 2 — The idea: one, and more than one (5–10 min)

Hold up one book, then several.

> "How many books is this?" *(one)* → **One.** &emsp; "And now?" *(several)* → **Many.**

Write two columns, no headings yet:

```
one              more than one
book             books
bird             birds
tree             trees
```

> "This side is **one** thing. This side is **more than one.**"
> "Listen to the naming word when there are many — *book… books.* What did you hear at the end?" → **An 's' sound.**

*The concept before the label — the child hears the change before naming it.*

### Step 3 — The term: singular / plural (10–14 min)

Head the columns.

| **Singular** *(one)* | **Plural** *(more than one)* |
|---|---|
| book | books |
| bird | birds |
| tree | trees |

> "One thing has a name: **singular.** Say it — *sin·gu·lar.*"
> "More than one has a name: **plural.** Say it — *plu·ral.*"
> "When we mean more than one, we say the **plural**. I give you one, you give me the plural — the *more-than-one* word."

**Term practice (~2 min):** choral read ×3 (*singular … plural*); teacher board-writes **plural**, class reads it back; post the Clue Card, read row 1 together.

> "Look at this word well today — **plural.**"

*First exposure — no recall demanded yet; Tuesday recalls it.*

**Teacher note.** *Plural* is the proper name for "more than one," so the child should own the word itself, not just the ending. *Singular* is taught as its partner so *plural* has a clear opposite. Keep the work on "give me the plural" — no need to drill "write the singular."

### Step 4 — The rule: most words add **s** (14–20 min)

Books closed. Go slowly — say the plural first, then name it, then the rule.

| Teacher says | Expected answer |
|---|---|
| "One book, two —?" | books |
| "One cat, five —?" | cats |
| "One tree, three —?" | trees |
| "One bird, many —?" | birds |
| "So the plural of *book* is —?" | books |
| "The plural of *cat* is —?" | cats |
| "Listen to the end — book, book**s.** What sound did we add?" | s |
| "So for most words, to make the plural, we add —?" | s |

Clue Card, row 1. **Held words for writing:** book, bird, tree, cat, stone, pebble, animal, hen, parrot, frog.

### Step 5 — Words ending s, sh, ch, x → **es** (20–26 min)

Word-cards, one at a time. Let them try the plural first.

| Teacher says | Expected answer |
|---|---|
| "One class, two —?" | *(may try 'class-s')* |
| "Listen — *class… classes.* One class, two —?" | classes |
| "One box, three —?" | boxes |
| "One watch, two —?" | watches |
| "So the plural of *class* is —?" | classes |
| "Say *class* with only *s* — *class-s.* Can you say it easily?" | No. |
| "That is why we add **es** — *classes.* Now you can hear it." | — |
| "Look at the last letter. Is it s, sh, ch, or x? Then we add —?" | es |

Clue Card, row 2. *(Block-local set: class, watch, box, dress, glass — gradeable in-block.)*

### Step 6 — One changer + CW-1 out (26–30 min)

| Teacher says | Expected answer |
|---|---|
| "One tooth, two —?" | *(may try 'tooths')* |
| "Listen — *tooth… teeth.* One tooth, two —?" | teeth |
| "So the plural of *tooth* is —?" | teeth |
| "Did we add *s*? No — the word changed inside. Some words we just remember." | — |

Clue Card, changers row. Distribute **CW-1**; model item 1 of each part, **including one singular** — *"I have one pen"* → *pen*, not *pens* — so the child sees "one" means leave it alone.

### Step 7 — Exit Check (30–35 min)

One prompt per student. Teacher says the singular; student says **and spells** the plural. *(Sized for 14 — confirm roll.)*

| 3 | tree | trees | | 10 | pebble | pebbles |

**Cue words (~1 min):**

| Teacher says | Expected answer |
|---|---|

### Step 2 — Term recall: plural (5–8 min)

**Books closed.** On the board, partial spelling: `plu____`

> "Finish the word — the name for *more than one.*" → **plural.**

Teacher completes it; class reads it back.

### Step 3 — The y-rule: consonant before y → **ies** (8–17 min)

| Teacher says | Expected answer |
|---|---|
| "One city, two —?" | *(may try 'citys')* |
| "Listen — *city… cities.* One city, two —?" | cities |
| "One story, three —?" | stories |
| "One family, two —?" | families |
| "So the plural of *city* is —?" | cities |

Write on the board: **cit y → cit ies**

| Teacher says | Expected answer |
|---|---|
| "Look at the letter just before the *y* in *city* — what is it?" | t |
| "Is *t* one of a, e, i, o, u?" | No. |
| "When the letter before *y* is not a, e, i, o, u — the *y* changes to **ies.**" | — |

Clue Card, y-row. **Held words:** city, story, family.

### Step 4 — The y-trap: a/e/i/o/u before y → just **s** (17–24 min)

Do it slowly — this catches children who over-apply the y-rule.

| Teacher says | Expected answer |
|---|---|
| "One day, two —?" | *(some say 'daies' — expected)* |
| "Listen — *day… days.* One day, two —?" | days |
| "One boy, three —?" | boys |
| "One monkey, two —?" | monkeys |

Put the two side by side: **cit y → cities** &emsp; **da y → days**

| Teacher says | Expected answer |
|---|---|
| "In *city*, the letter before y is *t* — not a, e, i, o, u. So y → **ies.**" | — |
| "In *day*, the letter before y is *a* — one of a, e, i, o, u! So we just add **s.**" | — |
| "*Monkey* — letter before y?" | e → just add s → monkeys |
| "*Story* — letter before y?" | r → not a, e, i, o, u → stories |

Clue Card, vowel-y note. **Held words:** boy, monkey. *(day taught as the trap; not a graded production target.)*
