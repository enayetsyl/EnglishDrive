## Three things I want your eye on

1. **Item 29 CW-3 — *"the bag of my cousin."*** English would more naturally say *my cousin's bag*. The block
   already grades this pattern (CW-3 A6, A13, A15; B23), so I have kept it **for consistency with what is already
   taught** — but if you would rather the possessive-*of* pattern were softened, it is a Part-B-wide change, not
   just this item, and I would bring that back as a separate proposal.

2. **Items 27/28 HW-1 use *into* as the distractor twice.** It is the clearest wrong-answer for a static slot, but
   two in five is repetitive. I can swap 28's distractor to *in* (*"The vehicle stops (at / in) the gate"*) — still
   forced, less repetitive. Your call.

3. **Open question from F5 that your ruling did not reach (Q3).** The clue **(together)** appears on five graded
   items — CW-3 B21/B26, HW-3 B21/B26, PT C6 — and `together` is a **W7** word. It is not incidental carrier text:
   it is the clue that tells the pupil to write *with*. Replace it with the English gloss **(with another person)**
   or the Preposition Clue Card's Bangla **(সঙ্গে)**? I recommend the Bangla — it is what a Sylhet Class 4 child
   will actually decode, and the card already carries it.
