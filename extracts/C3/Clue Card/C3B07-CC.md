| An adjective… | Ask… | Example (adjective **bold**) | বাংলা |
|---|---|---|---|
| **describes a naming word** | — | a **clean** hand · a **bright** morning | বিশেষ্যকে বর্ণনা করে |
| tells **what kind** | What kind of ___? | a **wet** road · a **cold** day | কেমন |
| tells **how many** | How many ___? | **two** books · **many** friends | কতগুলো |
| tells **which one** | Which ___? | **this** book · **that** boy | কোনটি |
| tells **whose** | Whose ___? | **my** bag · **her** book | কার |

> **The test is the job, not the place.** To find an adjective, ask: *does this word describe the noun — tell us what kind, how many, which, or whose?*

**Dual-job reminder:** a word's role comes from its **job here, in this sentence**. *clean* describing a noun = adjective (*a clean hand*); *clean* telling someone to do = verb (*clean the room*).
