  by W7). No word outside the pool appears, including in carrier position.
- **Simple present only.** No negatives, no questions (not in the §2 row-7 cap).
- **Pronoun subjects are carrier text**, never a trigger (PD-056).
- **§H.4 non-mahram pairing** — every two-person subject is same-gender or a mahram relation.
- **§H.3** — no sacred word is a graded target anywhere in this bank.
- **Attribution screen (CR-004 / CR-023)** — no natural phenomenon is an autonomous giver. Natural-world lines
  are descriptive (*flows*, *grows*, *blooms*), never *gives/provides*.

  Fatima, Porshi, Rabab, Jesmin.
- **Optative verbs excluded** — `forgive · grant · bless · protect · reward` appear nowhere; they are held
  only in the `May Allah ___` base frame and inflecting them collides with §H.3.
- **Every line is unique across the whole block** (PD-036, threshold 0). This bank is tracked as one corpus.
