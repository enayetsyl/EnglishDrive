# Class 5 English — Homework 1: *a* and *an* by sound

**School for Community Development — Sylhet Branch**
**Class 5 · English · Week 5 · Block 6: Article**

**Name:** ___________________ **Date:** ___________

> **Remember:** Say the word aloud. Listen to the **first sound**, not the first letter. Vowel sound → **an**. Consonant sound → **a**.

**Part A — Fill in the blank with *a* or *an*.** *(10 items, 1 mark each)*

1. The trip to the museum took ______ hour.
2. Aisha planted ______ flower in the garden.
3. Abdullah is ______ honest child.
4. Yusuf saw ______ tiger near the forest.
5. Aisha sent ______ email to her sister.
6. The council chose ______ candidate from our district.
7. We waited for ______ announcement about the race.
8. Abdullah wrote ______ poem about gratitude.
9. My parent works at ______ university.
10. The reporter described ______ election in Kishoreganj.

**Part B — Fill in the blank, then write why in two or three words.** *(8 items, 2 marks each)*

11. The champion is ______ hero to every child. **Why:** _______________
12. The reporter wrote about ______ European country. **Why:** _______________
13. The race started ______ hour after the announcement. **Why:** _______________
14. The child is ______ heir of a large library. **Why:** _______________
15. Abdullah read ______ historical storybook. **Why:** _______________
16. The forest has ______ one-eyed deer. **Why:** _______________
17. Yusuf saw ______ animal near the wall. **Why:** _______________
18. Aisha made ______ polite request. **Why:** _______________

**Part C — Write *a* or *an* before each.** *(6 items, 1 mark each)*

19. ______ island  20. ______ heir  21. ______ hero
22. ______ historical fort  23. ______ animal  24. ______ election

**Part D — One article in each sentence is wrong. Write it correctly.** *(6 items, 1 mark each)*

25. Abdullah is an brave child. ______
26. The bus reached the station in a hour. ______
27. Aisha is a honest reader. ______
28. We saw an tiger in the forest. ______
29. Yusuf borrowed an storybook from the library. ______
30. The council made a announcement about the election. ______
