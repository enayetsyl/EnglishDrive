## 4. §5A — corrections rules that bind this sheet set

Re-read before any drafting, as required. The ones that bite here:

- **CR-020 (strict alternation in a binary answer set) — the single biggest risk in this block.** SVA
  choose-the-form is inherently binary (`walk/walks`, `is/are`). At C2B06b, 15 answer sets ran
  `has·have·has·have…` and **108 of 275 marks were winnable by alternating without applying the rule**.
  `gate_depattern()` catches run>2 and strict alternation, but a near-alternating set can still pass.
- **CR-016 / CR-019 (a clue must not disclose the graded answer)** — interacts directly with **PD-054**.
  A Bangla gloss on an SVA item could hand over the number of the subject. Clues here need care or omission.
- **CR-032 (graded vocabulary never checked — no `trigger` declared), 3rd occurrence of the blind-spot type.**
  SVA answers are *verb forms*, not content words, exactly like C3's article answers. Every graded item needs
  a declared `trigger` or the held-word gate goes vacuously green over the whole block.
- **CR-001 / CR-030 (unheld word graded)** — see the pronoun problem in §5 below.
- **CR-004 / CR-023 (attribution)**, **CR-006** (HW key not transcribable), **CR-008 / CR-017** (option lists),
  **CR-009** (cross-sheet repetition, threshold 0), **CR-010** (naturalness is human), **CR-011 → PD-054**
  (Bangla glosses), **CR-002** (PT zero-overlap incl. unsat PTs), **CR-007** (verify every PD citation at
  source — `audits/scripts/check_citations.py`).

## 5. Flags raised — Phase 2 questions, blocking

1. **Pronouns are not in File 2 at all, and the cap requires them.** The cap names *He goes (not He go)* and
   *is/are*. `he · she · it · they · we` are function words, excluded from the pool at source
   (`Overview!B16`). They are taught as structure in **B5 (W5)**, but the **held-word gate keys on `trigger`**,
   so any SVA item with a pronoun subject fails unless declared. **C3B08 hit this exact wall two days ago and
   the Principal ruled PD-051** (eleven personal pronouns declared block-local). **PD-052 explicitly flagged
   cross-class replication to C1/C2/C4/C5 as NOT executed.** C4B07 needs its own declaration.
2. **`each + singular` is in the cap; `each` is a function word and `student` is not in the pool.** The HY25
   anchor item is `Each of the students ___ (has/have)`. Needs a block-local or exemplar route.
3. **The two anchors disagree on mark weighting** — HY25 Q5 is 5×2=10, Annual Q7 is 5×1=5, same format. Which
   does the PT mirror?
4. **Annual Q7(b) breaks its own format:** `English ___ (be)` supplies a single base verb, not a slash pair,
   and `English` is a proper noun (excluded from the pool). Mirror the slash-pair only, or carry the `(be)`
   variant too?
5. **HY26 Q8 is malformed as printed** — names two tenses but supplies no bracketed verb and no word bank.
   Reported as-is; it is not a Block 7 anchor, so it gates nothing here.
6. **Dual-job POS ambiguity is a live one-defensible-answer risk.** Held verbs that are common nouns
   (`play · help · like · visit · drink · love · care · share · cover · fill`) and held nouns that are common
   verbs (`rain · form · stamp · park · picture · hand · smoke · waste · card · stone`).
7. **§H.8** bars living-being imagery on graded sheets — `bird`, `dog` are fine as words, not as pictures.
   The Annual anchor item `Birds ___ (build/builds) nests` is text, so permitted.
