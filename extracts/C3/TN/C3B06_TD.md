**SCHOOL FOR COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT**

**Class 3 — English Grammar Campaign**

**Teacher Delivery Sheet — Block 06: Article (a / an / the)**

**Runs:** Week 5 · 4 teaching days (Sun–Wed) + Performance Test (Thu) · 35 min/session

---

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the block, students can:

- Say what an article is and that it comes **before a noun**.
- Choose **a** or **an** by listening to the first **sound** of the next word — including *hour, honest, university*.
- Use **the** for a noun that is the one we mean, the only one of its kind, or a whole kind.
- Find a wrong article in a sentence and correct it, saying why.
- Build their own correct sentence with an article and give a reason.

**Teach only to the cap:** *a/an* by sound; *the* for specific / unique / whole kind. **No zero-article rules. No abstract definiteness theory.**

These words exist to demonstrate the *a/an* sound rule. Say every one aloud before writing it — the ear leads. Nothing is graded before its teaching day.

## Sacred-word guard

The HY paper includes *"The Quran is a holy book."* Students answer it using the **same rule practised on *the cow / the ostrich*** — generic *the* for a whole kind, *a* to classify. You may read that exam sentence aloud **reverently** in oral preparation so students recognise it. It is **not** set as a worksheet or Performance Test blank in these materials. *Quran* appears only as a reverent subject, never as a fill-in target.

## Day 1 (Sunday) — Articles, and *a / an* by sound

**Objective.** Students know what an article is and use *a* and *an* correctly by listening to the first **sound** of the next word.

**Words today:** owl · ostrich · orange · egg · apple · umbrella · hour · honest · university

1. **What is an article? (0–8).** Recall Block 3 briefly. Write: `book · Yusuf · sister` — "Which are common nouns? Which is a name?" → *book, sister* common; *Yusuf* a name. Now write:

   ```
       book        a book        the book
   ```

   "*Book* is just the word. **A book** — any one, we do not know which. **The book** — the one we mean, the one we already know." Then: "These little words — **a, an, the** — are **articles**. An article comes **before a noun**." Leave on the board:

   ```
   a, an  →  any one
   the    →  the one we mean
   ```

   Close: "This week: today, **a** and **an**. Tomorrow, **the**."

2. **Listen to the sound (8–18).** "You know **an** from an earlier class. When do we use it?" Someone says *a, e, i, o, u* — write it up. "Good — the five vowel letters. That rule is right most of the time. Keep it. But the real secret is **sound**, not letters." Books closed; say words, class gives the article chorally: `book · apple · friend · egg · owl · teacher · umbrella · card · orange · hand`. Write the rule and read it together: **an** before a vowel sound · **a** before a consonant sound.

3. **Three tricky words (18–25).** "Now three words where the eye and the ear disagree. Books closed, listen."

   | We write | We say | Article | Why |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | hour | *our* | **an hour** | the *h* is silent → vowel sound |
   | honest | *onest* | **an honest boy** | the *h* is silent → vowel sound |
   | university | *yu-…* | **a university** | starts with a *yu* sound → consonant |

   "The **sound** decides, not the letter. Your ears decide, not your eyes." Quick oral round mixing plain and tricky: `apple · hour · book · university · egg · honest boy · friend · owl`.

4. **CW-1 (25–30).** *(CW-1 is on its own sheet.)* Model Part A item 1 on the board. Students work as far as they can; the rest goes home as HW-1.

5. **Exit Check (30–35).** Teacher says the word; **one student per prompt (17)** gives article + reason.

   | # | Prompt | Ans | # | Prompt | Ans |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | book | a | 10 | egg | an |
   | 2 | apple | an | 11 | university | a |
   | 3 | owl | an | 12 | hand | a |
   | 4 | friend | a | 13 | orange | an |
   | 5 | hour | an | 14 | card | a |
   | 6 | teacher | a | 15 | umbrella | an |
   | 7 | ostrich | an | 16 | library | a |
   | 8 | honest boy | an | 17 | egg | an |
   | 9 | card | a | | | |

## Day 2 (Monday) — *the*

**Objective.** Students use *the* for a noun that is the one we mean — already named, or the only one of its kind — and choose between *a/an* and *the*.

**Words today:** none new. *the sun · the Padma · the sky* are teaching examples.

1. **Recall, then meet *the* (0–6).** Books closed, quick oral round — *a* or *an*: `apple · book · hour · egg · university · friend · owl`. Then write and read aloud:

   ```
   Yusuf gave me a pen. The pen is green.
   ```

   "First time — **a** pen, we did not know which one. Second time — **the** pen, now we know exactly which one. Once we know which one, we say **the**."

2. **When we use *the* (6–15).** Build three cases with the class. Keep it plain — no theory. "We use **the** when we mean **the one we mean** — not just any one."

   | We use *the* when… | Example |
   |---|---|
   | we already named it | I saw a bird. **The** bird was small. |
   | we say **which one** | **the** book on my desk |
   | there is **only one** | **the** sun · **the** moon · **the** sky · **the** Padma · **the** Jamuna · **the** Meghna |

   "The sun — how many suns are there? One. So we always say **the sun**. The moon, the sky — the same. The Padma, the Jamuna, the Meghna — each is one big river with that name, so each takes **the**." Read the "only one" row aloud together twice.

   *Keep the "only one" idea concrete (sun, sky); do not drift into abstract definiteness.*

3. **a/an or the? (15–25).** Write pairs; class chooses and says why:

   | Sentence | Answer | Why |
   |---|---|---|
   | I have ___ book. | a | any one |
   | ___ book on my desk is new. | the | which one |
   | We saw ___ owl. | an | any one, vowel sound |
   | ___ sun is bright today. | the | only one |
   | Rani ate ___ apple. | an | any one, vowel sound |
   | I bought ___ pen. ___ pen is red. | a … the | first any / then the one we mean |

   "Ask yourself: **do I mean any one, or the one we mean?**"

4. **CW-2 (25–30).** *(CW-2 is on its own sheet.)* Model item 1. The rest goes home as HW-2.

5. **Exit Check (30–35).** Teacher reads the sentence; **one student per prompt (17)** gives the article.

   | # | Prompt | Ans | # | Prompt | Ans |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | ___ sun rises in the east | the | 10 | I ate ___ orange | an |
   | 2 | I saw ___ bird | a | 11 | ___ Padma is a big river | the |
   | 3 | ___ apple is sweet (this one) | the | 12 | Yusuf has ___ kite | a |
   | 4 | We have ___ egg | an | 13 | ___ sky is blue | the |
   | 5 | ___ book on the table is mine | the | 14 | I met ___ friend | a |
   | 6 | She has ___ umbrella | an | 15 | ___ owl in the tree is white | the |
   | 7 | ___ moon is far away | the | 16 | He saw ___ ostrich | an |
   | 8 | Give me ___ card | a | 17 | ___ hand is clean | the |
   | 9 | ___ hour has passed | an | | | |

## Day 3 (Tuesday) — *a / an / the* together (+ *the* for a whole kind)

**Objective.** Students choose among all three articles in mixed sentences, sort by reason across **three** *the*-jobs, and produce generic *the* for a whole kind.

**Words today:** onion · ant · olive (running total 12)

1. **Recall + three new words (0–5).** Rapid oral round, all three articles: `___ sun · ___ book · ___ egg · ___ university · ___ Padma`. Teach today's words aloud (ear first):

   | Word | Say | Article |
   |---|---|---|
   | onion | *un-yun* | an onion |
   | ant | *ant* | an ant |
   | olive | *o-liv* | an olive |

   "All three start with a vowel sound — so all take **an**."

2. ***the* for a whole kind (5–12) — new.** "You know two jobs for **the**: the only one (**the sun**), and the one we mean (**the book on my desk**). Today, one more job." Write:

   ```
   The cow is a useful animal.
   The ostrich is a big bird.
   ```

   "Here **the cow** does not mean one special cow. It means **cows** — the whole kind, all of them. When we talk about a **whole kind** of animal or thing, we use **the**." Then: "And after it — *is **a** useful animal* — that **a** just tells what kind of thing it is. Consonant sound, so **a**."

   Build two or three together, students producing the *the*:

   | Whole kind | Say it |
   |---|---|
   | ___ ox is ___ strong animal. | The ox is a strong animal. |
   | ___ ostrich is ___ big bird. | The ostrich is a big bird. |
   | ___ owl is ___ night bird. | The owl is a night bird. |

   "Three jobs for **the** now: **the only one**, **the one we mean**, **the whole kind**. All three — **the**."

   *Exam fidelity (sacred-word guard) — see the box at the top of this sheet.*

3. **Sort by reason, three *the*-jobs (12–18).** Four columns now:

   ```
    a / an (any one) | the (only one) | the (the one we mean) | the (whole kind)
   ```

   Items to sort (call out, class directs): `a friend · the sun · an onion · the cow (all cows) · the book on my desk · an ant · the sky · the ostrich (all ostriches) · an egg · the Padma`.

   "Two easy checks first: any one → **a/an**. Only one in the world → **the**. Then the two harder **the**s: the one I mean, or the whole kind."

4. **Mixed practice (18–25).**

   | Sentence | Answer | Why |
   |---|---|---|
   | ___ cow is ___ useful animal. | The … a | taught pattern — general truth |
   | ___ sun gives us light by the grace of Allah. | The | only one |
   | Mother cuts ___ onion. | an | any one, vowel sound |
   | ___ boy in class three is my friend. | the | which one |
   | ___ ostrich is ___ big bird. | The … a | whole kind / classifier |
   | Yusuf ate ___ olive and ___ apple. | an … an | both vowel sounds |

   *No living-being imagery for the animals; the words stand alone. In "a useful animal" / "a big bird", watch the sound — *useful* begins with a "yu" sound → **a**; *big* is a plain consonant → **a**.*

5. **CW-3 (25–30).** *(CW-3 is on its own sheet.)* Model item 1. The rest goes home as HW-3.

6. **Exit Check (30–35).** Teacher reads; **17 prompts**, article + one-word reason. Now includes whole-kind items.

   | # | Prompt | Ans | # | Prompt | Ans |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | ___ onion | an | 10 | ___ sky | the |
   | 2 | ___ book on my desk | the | 11 | ___ ant | an |
   | 3 | ___ friend | a | 12 | ___ olive | an |
   | 4 | ___ cow (all cows) | the | 13 | ___ teacher | a |
   | 5 | ___ egg | an | 14 | ___ Padma | the |
   | 6 | ___ orange | an | 15 | ___ ostrich (all) | the |

## Day 4 (Wednesday) — Mixed practice, error-find, Self-try demonstration

**Objective.** Students find a wrong article in a sentence and correct it, then build their own correct sentence.

**Words today:** elephant · ox (running total 14)

1. **Recall + two new words (0–5).** Rapid round, all three articles. Then:

   | Word | Say | Article |
   |---|---|---|
   | elephant | *e-le-phant* | an elephant |
   | ox | *ox* | an ox |

   "Both start with a vowel sound → **an**."

2. **Find the wrong article (5–16).** Write sentences with one wrong article each. Class finds it, fixes it, says why.

   | Wrong | Fix | Why |
   |---|---|---|
   | I saw a owl. | **an** owl | vowel sound |
   | An book is on the desk. | **A** book | consonant sound |
   | Sun is bright today. | **The** sun | only one |
   | Rani ate a egg. | **an** egg | vowel sound |
   | I waited an hour. ✓ | *(correct — trap check)* | silent *h* |
   | He has a university bag. ✓ | *(correct — trap check)* | *yu* sound |

   "Read it aloud. If your ear says a different article, that is the mistake."

   *Two items are already correct (*an hour*, *a university*) — a deliberate check that students do not "fix" what is right. Praise the ones who leave them alone.*

3. **Self-try — demonstration (16–25). Not collected.** The graded Self-try is tomorrow's Performance Test. Model on the board first:

   > "Watch me. I choose a word — **apple**. It starts with a vowel sound, so — **an**. My sentence: *I ate an apple after Maghrib.* I picked the article, then wrote a whole sentence."

   Give the frame:

   ```
   (a) My word: ______      (b) Article: ___      (c) My sentence: __________
   (d) Why this article? __________
   ```

   Students try one at their desks; take three or four aloud. Fix gently, celebrate correct reasoning.

   *Tomorrow's Performance Test Self-try uses a **different** word box, so a rehearsed sentence will not transfer — the reasoning is what carries over, not the sentence.*

4. **CW-4 (25–30).** *(CW-4 is on its own sheet; HW-4 likewise.)* Model Part A item 1. The rest goes home as HW-4.

5. **Exit Check (30–35).** Mixed — some "give the article," some "is this right?" **17 prompts.**

   | # | Prompt | Ans | # | Prompt | Ans |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | ___ elephant | an | 10 | *a egg* — fix | an egg |
   | 2 | ___ book | a | 11 | ___ ox | an |
   | 3 | ___ sun | the | 12 | ___ friend | a |
   | 4 | *a owl* — fix | an owl | 13 | ___ orange | an |
   | 5 | ___ hour | an | 14 | *sun* — add article | the sun |
   | 6 | ___ card | a | 15 | ___ apple | an |
   | 7 | ___ Padma | the | 16 | ___ hand | a |
   | 8 | ___ university | a | 17 | ___ egg | an |
   | 9 | ___ onion | an | | | |

## Article Clue Card (`C3B06-CC`)

Built with the class across the week; on the wall by Thursday. *(Also issued as its own sheet for the wall.)*

> **WHICH ARTICLE?**
>
> **1. Do we know which one? Is there only one?** → **the**
> *(the sun · the Padma · the sky)*
>
> **2. Any one — not a particular one?** → **a / an**
> **Listen to the first SOUND, not the letter.**
>
> | Vowel sound → **an** | Consonant sound → **a** |
> |---|---|
> | an apple, an egg, an owl | a book, a friend, a teacher |
> | an hour *(h silent)* | a university *(sounds like "yu")* |
> | an honest boy *(h silent)* | a one-taka note *(sounds like "wan")* |
>
> **Say the word aloud. Your ears decide, not your eyes.**

## Performance Test — how to run it (Thursday)

*(The student test is on its own sheet, `C3B06-PT`. Full Marks 34. In-school, teacher-administered.)*

- **Part A — Fill in suitable articles [10].** The binding paper's own wording. Eight sentences; some carry two blanks.
- **Part B — Article before a single word [5].**
- **Part C — Fill in, then write why in two or three words [5×2=10].** The reason line carries half the marks here — remind students before they start that "why" must be answered, not left blank.
- **Part D — Find the wrong article; some are already correct [5].** Two items are correct. Do not hint which.
- **Part E — Self-construction (free-thinking task) [4].** Word box: `egg · kite · onion · sun`. **Deliberately different from Wednesday's demonstration**, so a rehearsed sentence does not transfer.

**Self-try rubric — 4 marks, 1 each:** content accuracy · reasoning · organisation · word-class placement. Each criterion scores 1 or 0 — no partial credit. **Value alignment is not scored** — a religiously neutral, on-topic sentence earns full marks.
