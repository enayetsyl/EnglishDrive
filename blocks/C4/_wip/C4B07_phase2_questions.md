## Q1 — Do the personal pronouns need a block-local declaration, as C3B08 got at PD-051?

**Recommendation: NO declaration. C4B07 does not replicate PD-051.** This resolves PD-052's "cross-class
replication to C1/C2/C4/C5 flagged NOT executed" in the negative *for C4*.

Three separate reasons, each sufficient:

1. **In C4B07 a pronoun is never the answer.** PD-051 declared C3's pronouns block-local because *"every keyed
   answer in the block is one of them"* — it was a Pronoun block. Here the keyed answer is always a **verb
   form** (`goes`, `are`). The pronoun is the **subject**, i.e. carrier text — and the held-word gate is
   verified on the **trigger**, not carrier text (CLAUDE.md §4). Structurally different case.
2. **`is/are` blanks are already covered by PD-026** — a function word that *is* the grammar target takes none
   of the three non-held statuses and passes §6.5 on that basis. That is exactly this block's `is/are` strand,
   and C4B05 already ran on the same footing with no PD-012 set.
3. **Pronouns are taught structure, held from B5 (W5)**, two weeks before this block.

**But the cap's own example verb fails.** *He goes* — **`go` is absent from the pool.** The fix is
**recast, not declare**: PD-035's boundary states incidental carriers are recast onto held words, and
declaration is reserved for cases where recasting would *delete the capped concept*. It would not here — the
concept is 3rd-person `-s/-es`, and 34 held verbs carry it, including the `-es/-ies` cases (`washes · mixes ·
finishes · discusses · carries`). *He goes* is illustrative wording in the cap, not a mandated lexical item.
So: **`He plays` / `She washes` / `The child carries`**, and *He goes* appears only in teacher script, which
Drive Plan §5 permits.

**Ruling needed because** it declines to replicate a ruling you made two days ago for the neighbouring class,
and PD-052 left C4 explicitly open.

## Q2 — `each + singular`: declare, or recast?

The cap names *each + singular*; the HY25 anchor is `Each of the students ___ (has/have)`. **`each` and
`student` are both absent from the pool.**

**Recommendation: recast, no declaration.** `each` is a closed-class grammar target covered by **PD-026** —
it *is* the structure under instruction, like `is/are`. The noun simply moves to held vocabulary:
**`Each of the children ___ (has / have)`** — `child` is held W3 and `children` is its taught Block-2 irregular
plural. Same for the other anchor subjects: `student → child/friend`, `baker → worker` (held W6),
`girl/boy → ` house roster names (Charter §H.5).

## Q3 — PT mark weighting: the two anchors disagree on an identical format

HY25 Q5 = **5×2 = 10**. Annual Q7 = **5×1 = 5**. Same item type, same instruction wording.

**Recommendation: 1 mark per item throughout** (worksheets and PT), and record HY25's 2× weighting in the
Teaching Days notes so the teacher knows the exam may weight the same item double. Reason: the mark value
changes nothing about the pupil's task, 1-per-item keeps every stated total trivially recomputable by the
marks gate, and the block's practice volume comes from item count, not weighting.

## Q4 — Annual Q7(b) `English ___ (be)` — mirror the deviation, or the dominant format?

Nine of the ten anchor items are two slash-separated options in brackets. Item (b) alone supplies a **single
base verb** `(be)` and takes a **proper noun** subject (`English`, excluded from the pool at source).

**Recommendation: mirror the slash-pair format only, and ensure `is / are` is represented on every sheet**
(it is named in the cap). The `(be)` variant is a one-off deviation in one paper; reproducing it would import
a proper-noun subject the pool cannot supply and give the block two competing item formats for one skill.
**Carry it once, in the PT only**, if you want pupils to have met it before the exam — say which.

## Q5 — Dual-job words on graded items

Held verbs that are also common nouns (`play · help · like · visit · drink · love · care · share · cover ·
fill`) and held nouns that are also common verbs (`rain · form · stamp · park · picture · hand · smoke ·
waste · card · stone`).

**Recommendation: no restriction on the SVA fill-in items** — the blank sits in the verb slot, so the frame
fixes the word class and only one answer is defensible. **Bar dual-job words from any word-class
identification item** in this block, where the ambiguity is real (this is the CR-008/CR-017 surface).
Resolving by default unless you say otherwise.
