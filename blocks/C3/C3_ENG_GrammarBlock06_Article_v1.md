## Article Clue Card — `C3B06-CC`

> **WHICH ARTICLE?**
>
> **1. Do we know which one? Is there only one?** → **the**
> *(the sun · the Padma · the sky)*
>
> **2. Any one — not a particular one?** → **a / an**
> **Listen to the first SOUND, not the letter.**
>
> | Vowel sound → **an** | Consonant sound → **a** |
> |---|---|
> | an apple, an egg, an owl | a book, a friend, a teacher |
> | an hour *(h silent)* | a university *(sounds like "yu")* |
> | an honest boy *(h silent)* | a one-taka note *(sounds like "wan")* |
>
> **Say the word aloud. Your ears decide, not your eyes.**

# Teaching Days

## Class 1 (Sunday) — Articles, and *a / an* by sound

**Objective.** Students know what an article is and use *a* and *an* correctly by listening to the first **sound** of the next word — including *hour, honest, university*.

**Block-local words today:** `owl · ostrich · orange · egg · apple · umbrella · hour · honest · university`

| Step | Time | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–8 | What is an article? *a / an / the* |
| 2 | 8–18 | *a* or *an* — listen to the sound |
| 3 | 18–25 | Three tricky words |
| 4 | 25–30 | CW-1 |
| 5 | 30–35 | Exit Check (17) |

### Step 1 — What is an article? (0–8 min)

Recall Block 3 briefly. Write: `book · Yusuf · sister`

> "Which are common nouns? Which is a name?" → *book, sister* common · *Yusuf* a name.

Now write:

```
    book        a book        the book
```

> "*Book* is just the word. **A book** — any one, we do not know which. **The book** — the one we mean, the one we already know."

> "These little words — **a, an, the** — are **articles**. An article comes **before a noun**."

Fix the two ideas simply, and leave them on the board:

```
a, an  →  any one
the    →  the one we mean
```

> "This week: today, **a** and **an**. Tomorrow, **the**."

### Step 2 — *a* or *an*? Listen to the sound (8–18 min)

> "You know **an** from an earlier class. When do we use it?"

Someone says *a, e, i, o, u*. Write it up.

> "Good — the five vowel letters. That rule is right most of the time. Keep it. But the real secret is **sound**, not letters. Say the word aloud and listen to how it starts."

Books closed. Say words; class gives the article chorally:

`book · apple · friend · egg · owl · teacher · umbrella · card · orange · hand`

> "**an** goes before a **vowel sound** — a, e, i, o, u sounds. **a** goes before a **consonant sound** — everything else."

Write the rule and read it together:

> **an** before a vowel sound · **a** before a consonant sound

### Step 3 — Three tricky words (18–25 min)

> "Now three words where the eye and the ear disagree. Books closed, listen."

Say each; let them guess, then fix:

| We write | We say | Article | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| hour | *our* | **an hour** | the *h* is silent → vowel sound |
| honest | *onest* | **an honest boy** | the *h* is silent → vowel sound |
| university | *yu-...* | **a university** | starts with a *yu* sound → consonant |

> "The **sound** decides, not the letter. Your ears decide, not your eyes."

Quick oral round mixing plain and tricky:
`apple · hour · book · university · egg · honest boy · friend · owl`

### Step 4 — CW-1 (25–30 min)

Distribute CW-1. Model Part A item 1 on the board. Students work as far as they can; the rest goes home as HW-1.

### Step 5 — Exit Check (30–35 min)

Teacher says the word; **one student per prompt (17)** gives article + reason. De-patterned (max run ≤2).

| # | Prompt | Answer | Reason | # | Prompt | Answer | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | book | a | consonant | 10 | egg | an | vowel |
| 2 | apple | an | vowel | 11 | university | a | *yu* sound |
| 3 | owl | an | vowel | 12 | hand | a | consonant |
| 4 | friend | a | consonant | 13 | orange | an | vowel |

## Class 2 (Monday) — *the*

**Objective.** Students use *the* for a noun that is the one we mean — already named, or the only one of its kind — and choose between *a/an* and *the*.

**Block-local words today:** none new (uses held nouns + *the sun · the Padma · the sky* as teaching examples)

| Step | Time | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–6 | Recall *a/an*; meet *the* |
| 2 | 6–15 | When we use *the* |
| 3 | 15–25 | *a/an* or *the*? |
| 4 | 25–30 | CW-2 |
| 5 | 30–35 | Exit Check (17) |

### Step 1 — Recall, then meet *the* (0–6 min)

Books closed, quick oral round — *a* or *an*:
`apple · book · hour · egg · university · friend · owl`

Then write and read aloud:

```
Yusuf gave me a pen. The pen is green.
```

> "First time — **a** pen, we did not know which one. Second time — **the** pen, now we know exactly which one. Once we know which one, we say **the**."

### Step 2 — When we use *the* (6–15 min)

Build three cases with the class. Keep it plain — no theory.

> "We use **the** when we mean **the one we mean** — not just any one."

| We use *the* when… | Example |
|---|---|
| we already named it | I saw a bird. **The** bird was small. |
| we say **which one** | **the** book on my desk |
| there is **only one** | **the** sun · **the** moon · **the** sky · **the** Padma · **the** Jamuna · **the** Meghna |

> "The sun — how many suns are there? One. So we always say **the sun**. The moon, the sky — the same. The Padma, the Jamuna, the Meghna — each is one big river with that name, so each takes **the**."

Read the "only one" row aloud together twice.

**Teacher note.** *the Padma, the Jamuna, the Meghna, the sun, the sky* are teaching examples — graded items use held nouns. Keep the "only one" idea concrete (sun, sky); do not drift into abstract definiteness.

### Step 3 — *a/an* or *the*? (15–25 min)

Write pairs; class chooses and says why:

| Sentence | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I have ___ book. | a | any one |
| ___ book on my desk is new. | the | which one |
| We saw ___ owl. | an | any one, vowel sound |
| ___ sun is bright today. | the | only one |
| Rani ate ___ apple. | an | any one, vowel sound |
| I bought ___ pen. ___ pen is red. | a … the | first any / then the one we mean |

> "Ask yourself: **do I mean any one, or the one we mean?**"

### Step 4 — CW-2 (25–30 min)

Distribute CW-2. Model item 1. The rest goes home as HW-2.

### Step 5 — Exit Check (30–35 min)

Teacher reads the sentence; **one student per prompt (17)** gives the article. De-patterned.

| # | Prompt | Ans | # | Prompt | Ans |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ___ sun rises in the east | the | 10 | I ate ___ orange | an |
| 2 | I saw ___ bird | a | 11 | ___ Padma is a big river | the |
| 3 | ___ apple is sweet (this one) | the | 12 | Yusuf has ___ kite | a |
| 4 | We have ___ egg | an | 13 | ___ sky is blue | the |
| 5 | ___ book on the table is mine | the | 14 | I met ___ friend | a |
| 6 | She has ___ umbrella | an | 15 | ___ owl in the tree is white | the |

## Class 3 (Tuesday) — *a / an / the* together (+ *the* for a whole kind)

**Objective.** Students choose among all three articles in mixed sentences, sort by reason across **three** *the*-jobs, and produce generic *the* for a whole kind.

**Block-local words today:** `onion · ant · olive` (cumulative 12)

| Step | Time | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–5 | Recall; three new words |
| 2 | 5–12 | *the* for a whole kind (new) |
| 3 | 12–18 | Sort by reason (three *the*-jobs) |
| 4 | 18–25 | Mixed practice |
| 5 | 25–30 | CW-3 |
| 6 | 30–35 | Exit Check (17) |

### Step 1 — Recall + three new words (0–5 min)

Rapid oral round, all three articles:
`___ sun · ___ book · ___ egg · ___ university · ___ Padma`

Teach today's three block-local words aloud (ear first):

| Word | Say | Article |
|---|---|---|
| onion | *un-yun* | an onion |
| ant | *ant* | an ant |
| olive | *o-liv* | an olive |

> "All three start with a vowel sound — so all take **an**."

### Step 2 — *the* for a whole kind (5–12 min)

> "You know two jobs for **the**: the only one (**the sun**), and the one we mean (**the book on my desk**). Today, one more job."

Write:

```
The cow is a useful animal.
The ostrich is a big bird.
```

> "Here **the cow** does not mean one special cow. It means **cows** — the whole kind, all of them. When we talk about a **whole kind** of animal or thing, we use **the**."

> "And after it — *is **a** useful animal* — that **a** just tells what kind of thing it is. Consonant sound, so **a**."

Build two or three together, students producing the *the*:

| Whole kind | Say it |
|---|---|
| ___ ox is ___ strong animal. | The ox is a strong animal. |
| ___ ostrich is ___ big bird. | The ostrich is a big bird. |
| ___ owl is ___ night bird. | The owl is a night bird. |

> "Three jobs for **the** now: **the only one**, **the one we mean**, **the whole kind**. All three — **the**."

**Teacher note — exam fidelity (sacred-word guard, §H.3).** The HY paper includes *"The Quran is a holy book."* Students answer it using the **same rule they practised on *the cow / the ostrich*** — generic *the* for a whole kind, *a* to classify. You may read that exam sentence aloud reverently during oral preparation so students recognise it; it is **not** set as a worksheet or PT blank in these materials. *Quran* appears only as a reverent subject, never as a fill-in target.

### Step 3 — Sort by reason, three *the*-jobs (12–18 min)

Four columns now:

```
 a / an (any one) | the (only one) | the (the one we mean) | the (whole kind)
```

Items to sort (call out, class directs):
`a friend · the sun · an onion · the cow (all cows) · the book on my desk · an ant · the sky · the ostrich (all ostriches) · an egg · the Padma`

> "Two easy checks first: any one → **a/an**. Only one in the world → **the**. Then the two harder **the**s: the one I mean, or the whole kind."

### Step 4 — Mixed practice (18–25 min)

| Sentence | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ___ cow is ___ useful animal. | The … a | *taught pattern — general truth (not graded for reasoning)*; *a* = *"yu"* sound |
| ___ sun gives us light by the grace of Allah. | The | only one |
| Mother cuts ___ onion. | an | any one, vowel sound |
| ___ boy in class three is my friend. | the | which one |
| ___ ostrich is ___ big bird. | The … a | whole kind / classifier |
| Yusuf ate ___ olive and ___ apple. | an … an | both vowel sounds |

**Teacher note.** No living-being imagery for the animals; the words stand alone. In *"a useful animal"* / *"a big bird"*, watch the sound — *useful* begins with a *"yu"* sound → **a**; *big* is a plain consonant → **a**.

### Step 5 — CW-3 (25–30 min)

Distribute CW-3. Model item 1. The rest goes home as HW-3.

### Step 6 — Exit Check (30–35 min)

Teacher reads; **17 prompts**, article + one-word reason. De-patterned. Now includes whole-kind items.

| # | Prompt | Ans | # | Prompt | Ans |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ___ onion | an | 10 | ___ sky | the |
| 2 | ___ book on my desk | the | 11 | ___ ant | an |
| 3 | ___ friend | a | 12 | ___ olive | an |
| 4 | ___ cow (all cows) | the | 13 | ___ teacher | a |
| 5 | ___ egg | an | 14 | ___ Padma | the |
| 6 | ___ orange | an | 15 | ___ ostrich (all) | the |

## Class 4 (Wednesday) — Mixed practice, error-find, Self-try (demonstration)

**Objective.** Students find a wrong article in a sentence and correct it (simple Analyze), then build their own correct sentence (guided Create — demonstration, not graded).

**Block-local words today:** `elephant · ox` (cumulative 14)

| Step | Time | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–5 | Recall; two new words |
| 2 | 5–16 | Find the wrong article |
| 3 | 16–25 | Self-try (demonstration) |
| 4 | 25–30 | CW-4 |
| 5 | 30–35 | Exit Check (17) |

### Step 1 — Recall + two new words (0–5 min)

Rapid round, all three articles. Then:

| Word | Say | Article |
|---|---|---|
| elephant | *e-le-phant* | an elephant |
| ox | *ox* | an ox |

> "Both start with a vowel sound → **an**."

### Step 2 — Find the wrong article (5–16 min)

Write sentences with one wrong article each. Class finds it, fixes it, says why.

| Wrong | Fix | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I saw a owl. | **an** owl | vowel sound |
| An book is on the desk. | **A** book | consonant sound |
| Sun is bright today. | **The** sun | only one |
| Rani ate a egg. | **an** egg | vowel sound |
| I waited an hour. ✓ | *(correct — trap check)* | silent *h* |
| He has a university bag. ✓ | *(correct — trap check)* | *yu* sound |

> "Read it aloud. If your ear says a different article, that is the mistake."

**Teacher note.** Two items are already correct (*an hour*, *a university*) — a deliberate check that students do not "fix" what is right. Praise the ones who leave them alone.

### Step 3 — Self-try — demonstration (16–25 min)

**This is a demonstration, not collected.** The graded Self-try is tomorrow's PT.

Model on the board first:

> "Watch me. I choose a word — **apple**. It starts with a vowel sound, so — **an**. My sentence: *I ate an apple after Maghrib.* I picked the article, then wrote a whole sentence."

Give the frame:

```
(a) My word: ______      (b) Article: ___      (c) My sentence: __________
(d) Why this article? __________
```

Students try one at their desks; take three or four aloud. Fix gently, celebrate correct reasoning.

**Teacher note.** Tomorrow's PT Self-try uses a **different** word box, so a rehearsed sentence will not transfer — the reasoning is what carries over, not the sentence.

### Step 4 — CW-4 (25–30 min)

Distribute CW-4. Model Part A item 1. The rest goes home as HW-4.

### Step 5 — Exit Check (30–35 min)

Mixed — some "give the article," some "is this right?" **17 prompts.**

| # | Prompt | Ans | # | Prompt | Ans |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ___ elephant | an | 10 | *a egg* — fix | an egg |
| 2 | ___ book | a | 11 | ___ ox | an |
| 3 | ___ sun | the | 12 | ___ friend | a |
| 4 | *a owl* — fix | an owl | 13 | ___ orange | an |

| 6 | ___ card | a | 15 | ___ apple | an |
