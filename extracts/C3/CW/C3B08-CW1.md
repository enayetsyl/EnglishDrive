> **Remember.** A **pronoun** takes the place of a naming word.
> Today's pronouns: **he · she · it · they**
> one boy or man → **he** · one girl or woman → **she** · one thing or animal → **it** · more than one → **they**
> **it** = one, but not a person — a thing, an animal, a place, a time.
