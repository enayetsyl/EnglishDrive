## ⚑ Finding that outranks everything else in this unit

**The `-es/-ies` sub-rule rests on exactly FIVE held verbs**, and there is no sixth anywhere in the 160-word
pool: `wash → washes` · `mix → mixes` · `finish → finishes` · `discuss → discusses` · `carry → carries`.
**`carry` is the only `-ies` verb in the entire pool.**

The cap names *3rd-person **-s/-es*** as a bolded requirement, so this is a Drive-Plan-mandated sub-rule
served by five words — and PD-036 forbids reusing a sentence anywhere in the block. Day 2's whole graded
surface must therefore be built by varying **subjects and objects** across five verb stems, and the `-ies`
half of the rule can only ever be demonstrated by `carry`.

This is the same shape as C3's missing size adjectives (CR-030 → PD-047): **a pool gap, not a defect in the
block.** I am **not** proposing a block-local declaration — recasting is not blocked here, the concept is
still teachable, and PD-035 reserves declaration for cases where recasting would delete the concept.
**Flagged for your awareness, not resolved.** If you want a wider `-es` set it is a File 2 change and needs
its own ruling.
