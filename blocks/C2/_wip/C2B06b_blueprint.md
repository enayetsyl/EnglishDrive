## 3. Scope and guardrails

**Taught and graded**

- **has** — third-person singular subjects (*he / she / it /* one naming word)
- **have** — third-person plural subjects (*they /* plural naming word)
- **match-to-make-a-sentence** — re-form five mis-paired sentences (subject + verb + complement)

**Taught orally, not graded** (ruling 3)

- ***I have · we have · you have*** — taught so children are not left wrong-footed in speech, but **no graded item tests first or second person.**

**Barred**

- *have got / has got* · negatives (*doesn't have*) · questions (*Does he have…?*)
- past / future / continuous · tense theory (carried from 6a)
- **sacred words in any match strip, worksheet or PT item** (PD-031)
- **unheld living beings — no *giraffe***; wild animals restricted to forest / tree / pond settings (carried from 6a)
- living-being **imagery** on any graded sheet (§H.8); non-mahram pairing (§H.4); music / festival lexicon
